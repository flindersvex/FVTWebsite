O'Week S1 2026

February 23-26, 2026

With the start of the new academic year comes the excitement of O'Week 2026, the biggest event for new and continuing students at Flinders University to get involved with clubs and meet new people! The VEX Robotics Team had been hard at work in the leading weeks building two new robots for our demo showcase, ready to demonstrate our skills and attract new members.

Fresh off our successes at the 2025 VEX Australian National Championships, we were excited to show off the bots and the game to the newest group of students. Our team members were on hand to answer questions, verse off against the public in some friendly matches, and help out new students looking to get involved in robotics.

With FVT setting up showcases in the New Venture Institute's brand-new Nucleus space at Bedford Park, the main Tonsley floor, and the Flinders City Campus the club was able to engage students across many disciplines: the club registered new members studying everything from archaeology to childhood education, as well as plenty from within our very own STEM programs!

Photo courtesy of FUSA

Across four days and three locations the club engaged with a great number of students from all experience levels, and is very happy to announce we welcomed over 20 new members to the team in this O'Week alone, with more expected to join in the coming weeks!

With the new year comes new opportunities, and the team is excited to see what 2026 has in store for us. With a new game, new members, and new challenges, we are looking forward to another successful year of robotics at Flinders University.
